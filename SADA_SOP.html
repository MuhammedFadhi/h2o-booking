The Procedure

One flow, end to end

Each stage below is a real step someone performs. The colour tag tells you whose job it is.

01
STAGE 01Office

New enquiryتسجيل عميل مهتم

Someone shows interest — a WhatsApp message, a call, a walk-in, or a referral. Log them so no lead is ever forgotten.

  • Capture their name and mobile number — the number is their identity, so get it exactly (+9665…).
  • Note what they're interested in and their area.
  • Move them along as you follow up: New → Contacted → Qualified.
02
STAGE 02Customer

Customer booksحجز الموعد والمنتجات

The customer opens the booking link and chooses what they want and when. They can also be booked in by the office on their behalf.

  • They build a basket — any mix of purifiers and a dispenser, each with its own quantity.
  • They pick a free date and time slot for their area.
  • They confirm. A booking reference is created and a confirmation SMS is sent automatically.
03
STAGE 03Office

Assign an installerإسناد الفني

In the dashboard, hand the booking to an installer who covers that region.

  • Open Bookings and find the new booking.
  • Assign an active installer for that area.
  • The customer and installer are both notified automatically.
04
STAGE 04Installer

Install & scan each unitالتركيب ومسح كل وحدة

On site, complete the job in the installer app. The app walks you through every unit, one at a time.

  • Fill and submit the inspection report first — the job can't be completed without it.
  • Take the installation photo.
  • Scan the QR on each purifier — the app guides you unit by unit (Unit 1 of 2, Unit 2 of 2…).
  • Submit to complete the job.
💧
One RO = one QR = one warranty. A dispenser has no QR.

Scan every purifier. Skip the water dispenser — it needs only the photo, no scan.

كل جهاز تنقية له رمز QR وضمان خاص به. جهاز التوزيع لا يحتاج مسح — فقط الصورة.

05
STAGE 05Automatic

Warranties activateتفعيل الضمان تلقائياً

The moment the job is marked complete, the system does the paperwork itself — no forms to fill.

  • Each scanned purifier becomes its own 24-month warranty.
  • The filter clock (90 days) and annual service clock start automatically.
  • Dispensers are recorded on the booking but carry no warranty.
06
STAGE 06Office

Lifetime serviceالخدمة الدورية

Keep every customer covered for years — reminders, filter changes, and annual service.

  • Send filter and service reminders when they come due.
  • Log each visit — the due-date clock resets from that date.
  • A customer who declines visits can be marked "no service"; their warranty stays valid.
Keep These Five

The rules everyone remembers

Small habits that keep the whole system clean — from the office desk to the customer's kitchen.

01

The number is the customer

Every record is tied to the mobile number. Always +9665…, always exact.

رقم الجوال هو هوية العميل — دائماً وبدقة.

02

Scan every purifier

One RO unit = one QR scan = one warranty. Two units means two scans.

كل جهاز تنقية = مسح واحد = ضمان واحد.

03

Dispensers ride free

No QR, no warranty — just the installation photo. Never invent a serial.

جهاز التوزيع بدون رمز أو ضمان — فقط الصورة.

04

Report before complete

No inspection report, no completion. Submit it first, every time.

لا إتمام بدون تقرير الفحص — قدّمه أولاً.

05

One booking, many units

A single visit can install several units. Scan every serialized one before you finish.

الحجز الواحد قد يشمل عدة وحدات — امسحها جميعاً.
